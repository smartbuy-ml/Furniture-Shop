# AURA & OAK — Modern & Timeless Furniture Shop Website

A modern, responsive, and feature-complete e-commerce website designed for high-end furniture retail. Built using vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript, ensuring zero build setup and instant opening in any modern web browser.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Curated Product Catalog & Details
- **14 Masterpiece Products across 5 Living Categories:**
  - **Living Room:** Astrid Velvet 3-Seater Sofa, Koto Minimalist Accent Armchair, Solstice Travertine Coffee Table
  - **Bedroom:** Bergen Solid Oak Platform Bed, Nordic Minimalist Nightstand, Elysian 6-Drawer Walnut Dresser
  - **Dining:** Neva Solid Oak Extendable Dining Table, Soren Spindle Dining Chairs, Valo Fluted Tambour Sideboard
  - **Workspace & Office:** Atelier Solid Walnut Executive Desk, ErgoCurve Leather Task Chair, Arcade Modular Oak Bookshelf
  - **Lighting & Decor:** Kyoto Ceramic Ribbed Table Lamp, Aalto Full-Length Solid Oak Floor Mirror
- **Detailed Specifications:** Each product displays dimensions, materials, joinery, weight capacity, warranty, and assembly information.
- **Product Quick View Modal:** Click any item to view large photo previews, color swatches, full description, technical specs table, quantity picker, and add directly to bag.

### 2. Interactive Search, Filters & Sorting
- **Real-Time Instant Search:** Filters live across furniture names, categories, and materials.
- **Category Filter Pills:** Switch effortlessly between All, Living Room, Bedroom, Dining, Office, and Lighting & Decor.
- **Sorting Engine:** Sort by Featured Picks, Price (Low to High), Price (High to Low), or Highest Customer Rating.

### 3. Shopping Cart Drawer & Shipping Calculator
- **Slide-Over Bag Drawer:** Smooth slide-in cart with thumbnail previews, selected finishes, quantity increment/decrement, and deletion controls.
- **Interactive Free Shipping Progress Bar:** Live calculation showing progress towards Free White-Glove Delivery ($500 threshold).
- **Voucher Promo Code Engine:** Apply promo code **`AURA10`** for 10% off your entire order with automatic real-time calculation.
- **LocalStorage Persistence:** Cart and wishlist items persist across browser page reloads.

### 4. Wishlist & Customer Experience
- **Interactive Wishlist Drawer:** Save favorite pieces with one click; move them into your cart anytime.
- **Simulated Checkout & Order Processing:** Fill in delivery address and payment method to receive a unique generated order confirmation code (e.g., `#AO-84920`).
- **Toast Notifications:** Feedback popups on adding to cart, saving to wishlist, or applying coupons.
- **Craftsmanship & Storytelling:** Highlights sustainable FSC timbers, traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery, and non-toxic plant wax finishes.
- **Verified Customer Reviews & FAQ Accordions.**
